# Deliverable Map — financial method (pilot)

**What this is:** an internal model that maps every dirham of a project to a **deliverable**, so (a) any output can be traced to a price in a dispute, and (b) the per-deliverable costs become a **reusable rate card** for future jobs. Distinct from the client-facing CP (which stays organised as a clean itemised cost).

**Method — "anchor + marginals":**
- The **anchor deliverable** (the EN hero master) carries **origination** — development + the shoot — because the shoot exists to create the film and can't be honestly split across aspect ratios.
- Every **other deliverable** is priced at its **marginal cost** — what it actually adds, and therefore what you credit if it's dropped or charge if it's added.
- Anchor + all marginals = the film total. No invented percentages.
- The marginals are the **reusable unit costs** (carry them to the next project); the anchor scales with shoot scale (days, worlds, crew, high-speed).
