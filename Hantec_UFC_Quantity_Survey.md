## 4. Shot-requirement dissection (drives crew, kit & schedule)

### Film 1 — Markets / Loma — "The Discipline to Decide" (premium, cinematic, *low movement*)
- Loma stretching; reviewing notes **with her coach** *(supporting talent: coach)*.
- "Watches others train" *(background fighters / extras)*.
- Close-ups of repetition, small movements, focus, patience *(macro / detail lensing)*.
- Trader reviewing charts, reading insights, "learning from others", waiting *(supporting talent: trader + screen content + background)*.
- Loma centre of gym, calm, hero frame; trader "makes a decision"; Loma looks ahead.
- Treatment note: **measured edit, longer holds, deliberate cuts, no fast montage**.
- **Constraint:** injury → static/posed/controlled action only, no sparring/impact.

### Film 2 — Trader C1 / Rei *or* Rinya — "Know Who's in Your Corner" (fast cuts, evidence-led)
- **Corner-team dynamic** *(supporting talent: cutman/coaches as a corner)*; talent seated/standing between rounds, calm.
- Close-up expressions; **direct-to-camera sign-off** *(requires clean sync sound on set)*.
- Intercut **trader at a screen** *(supporting talent + screen content)*.
- Evidence/claim beats: "in the markets since 1990", "35 years. Four continents. Zero payout freezes", payout-record references, news/weekend/min-days/payout conditions → **all are regulated financial-promotion claims** (see §5/RFI).

### Film 3 — Trader C2 / Rei *or* Rinya — "Every Trader Has a Signature Move" (energy builds, **high-speed**)
- **Six distinct strike captures, clean & individual:** Jab, Cross, Superman Punch, Hook, plus **Jab→Cross** combo and full **Jab→Cross→Hook** combo.
- Reset / reaction shots for editorial flexibility.
- **Slow-motion inserts on key strikes** → **high-speed camera** + extra lighting.
- Fighter wide stance; intercut trader at screen.
- Strike-to-product map (Instant Lite / Instant24 / Instant Funding / Express / Enhanced / Endurance) handled as **on-screen text in post** ("snap in on impact").

> **Schedule pressure:** 6-hr cap per fighter must absorb that fighter's **film + photo shoot**. Film 3's six clean strikes + combos + slow-mo is the tightest day → block carefully; consider a 2nd camera (sync + high-speed) rather than re-rigs.

## 5. Compliance flag (raise early — affects creative & cost)

Hantec is FSC-regulated; the scripts carry **financial-promotion claims** ("Trade with confidence", "Risk comes first. Protect first. Perform second.", "Zero payout freezes", "Keep the majority of what you earn", "35 years / Four continents"). These typically require:
- **Risk warnings / disclaimers** (on-screen + possibly VO) per market/platform.
- **Legal & compliance sign-off** woven into the 3 approval rounds (client + UFC + likely client compliance).
- Verifiable substantiation of claims (payout records etc.) — client to supply.

**Action:** carry on-screen **disclaimer/risk-warning design + localization** as explicit post line items, and add a compliance-review checkpoint to the schedule. **RFI-2:** confirm required disclaimer wording per market (TH/JP/global).
