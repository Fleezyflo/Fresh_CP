## Basis of estimate

- **Three films, each a standalone shoot.** Dates are fighter-led and cannot be promised, so **no bundling/shared-day saving is assumed** — every film carries its own full mobilisation.
- **This is the budget-aligned build** — engineered to the client's **$55–60k/film** through explicit scope decisions (§4), not rate-cutting. At full premium spec the same job is ~$261k (~$87–93k/film); this version is **~$178k** and lands each film inside the envelope.
- **Venue assumed provided via the UFC partnership.** The client is already exploring a UFC gym; as official UFC partners they can most likely secure a UFC-affiliated gym / Performance Institute environment at little or no cost. We carry the permit, fixer and holding. *(If the venue is not provided, add ~$5k/film — see §5.)*
- **Talent excluded** (client's): fighter fees, flights, travel, accommodation, UFC rights/scheduling, copy. We carry everything else including supporting cast and on-set talent care.
- **VAT (UAE 5%) excluded**, added at invoice. Figures USD.

> **Headline:** **~$178k total — F1 ~$58.5k · F2 ~$59.5k · F3 ~$60k.** That's ~$80k below the full-premium spec, achieved through the six scope decisions in §4. The trade-offs and the one real risk (venue) are in §5.

## 1. Deliverables (master-led, per film)

One finished **master** (30s hero, 16:9 — edit, grade, sound, music, EN VO, graphics); derive **9:16 + 1:1 reframes**, **15s + 6s cut-downs**, and the **localised version** (TH / JP — VO + on-screen text swapped on the master); plus a **retouched stills set**. On-screen text is one graphics layer carried across all versions. **Deliverable matrix trimmed** to the priority ratio/duration set per platform (see §4) rather than every possible combination.

## 3. Where the cost sits

Per film: **crew labour ~33%**, **post ~19%**, **equipment ~14%**, **art/cast/materials ~15%**, **prep + management ~14%**, location ~3% (venue provided), contingency 10%. Crew and post remain the two biggest blocks — they just aren't over-built now.

---

## 4. How we hit budget — the ~$80k bridge (full premium → this)

Every saving below is a **deliberate scope/structure decision**, not a discount or a favour rate:

| Decision | Approx. saving (all 3 films) | What changes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Venue via UFC partnership** (we carry permit/fixer only) | ~17k | Relies on client/UFC securing the gym — the one real dependency |
| **Single-camera, lean crew** (~12 vs ~18 pax: drop DIT, 2nd AC, dedicated gimbal op, BTS, photo assistant, set dresser, wardrobe assistant; combine roles) | ~14k | Tighter day, less simultaneous coverage |
| **Leaner post** (one grade pass, lighter mograph/versioning — **3 approval rounds retained** as the client requires) | ~14k | Less finishing headroom; 4th+ round chargeable |
| **No Phantom — high-fps on the main cinema body** (F3) + right-sized kit across all | ~9k | Good slow-mo, not extreme-fps slow-mo |
| **Right-sized prep, management, materials, cast & support** | ~18k | Efficient rather than generous loadings |
| **Trimmed deliverable matrix** + library music + lower contingency base | ~8k | Fewer paid-media variants; confirm exact deliverable list |
| **Total** | **~80k** | $261k → ~$178k |

## 5. Trade-offs & the one risk that matters

**Trade-offs accepted to hit budget:** single-camera coverage (disciplined shot-listing within the 6-hr cap matters more); good rather than extreme slow-mo on F3; an efficient deliverable set rather than every ratio/duration permutation; finishing headroom is tighter though all three approval rounds are funded.

**The risk to flag clearly: the venue.** ~$17k of the saving assumes the UFC/client provides the gym. If they can't, location returns at **~$5k/film (~+$15k)** and we're back near the top of the envelope — so this needs confirming early. As UFC partners it should be very achievable, and we can help source/recommend (we offered).

> If the client wants a hard guarantee regardless of the venue outcome, the honest options are: (a) accept ~$60–63k/film if we must rent the venue, or (b) take a further trim from the deliverable matrix / F3 action package to absorb it.

## 6. Inclusions / exclusions / assumptions

**Included:** lean premium crew, equipment, permit/fixer, art/set, wardrobe, supporting cast + 6-month usage, full master-led post with 3 approval rounds, one localised language/film, stills, insurance, transport, catering, contingency.

**Excluded (client or add-on):** fighter fees/flights/travel/accommodation; UFC rights & guidelines; copy; **venue rental** (assumed partnership-provided); UAE VAT; bespoke music (+~5–8k); extra languages beyond one/film; 4th+ approval rounds; second camera / Phantom if extreme slow-mo is wanted on F3 (+~4–5k); crew/director travel if not Dubai-based (assumed local).

**Assumptions:** all 3 films proceed (Rei/Rinya split, cost-neutral); one fighter per standalone shoot day within the 6-hr cap; native VO for TH/JP; venue provided via UFC partnership; ~8–10 retouched stills/fighter; on-screen text and language versions produced in post from the EN master; agreed trims (dresser into art dept, no wardrobe assistant) retained.
