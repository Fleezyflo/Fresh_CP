## 1. Deliverables — "The System" (one shoot, hall + feed)
- **Screen master** — 90s, with a **60s alternate**. Format-agnostic (cut to hold any stage screen).
- **Social** — a cut **per verse (×6)**, in **9:16 and 4:5**.
- **Trims** — **30s, 15s, 6s**, each carrying one verse.
- **Connective type** — the poem set as the type system across all outputs.
- Bilingual throughout: **Arabic leads, English beside it** (equal, not a subtitle trailing).

## 5. Flexibility — how this scales
- **Director route** — Lead vs Alternate is the largest single lever (~AED 50k); pass-through means it never affects our fee.
- **Shoot days** — priced at **2 days** (golden-hour available-light windows + multi-generational cast across several setups). A disciplined **1-day** shoot removes ~AED 95–105k but tightens coverage and risks the light.
- **Deliverable system** — the full system (master + 60s + 6 verse cuts ×2 ratios + 30/15/6 trims) is costed; trimming the social/trim matrix is a clean post-only saving.
- **Poem & score** — both can be quoted as transparent pass-throughs too (like the director) if you'd prefer that treatment for the credited artists.

## 6. Exclusions & variations (each available as a priced variation)
- Fees, travel and logistics for any client-side or DWE-network talent beyond the casting allowance.

- Additional shoot days, additional languages beyond AR + EN, or a second location.
- Hospitality/screening-event production at the forum itself.
- VAT — excluded, added per UAE law.
